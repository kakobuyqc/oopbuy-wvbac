03 / Oopbuy shopping guide

A useful find deserves a closer look.

An Oopbuy product list can help you discover options. A consistent comparison process helps you decide which options deserve your attention.

Build your own shortlist ↗
  1. 01

    Define what you need

    Choose a category, intended use, preferred fit and spending limit before comparing individual listings.

  2. 02

    Check the actual listing

    Read the variant, measurements, fabric or material description, seller information and return conditions.

  3. 03

    Review photos critically

    Where available, examine item-specific quality check photos. Look at labels, stitching, shape and visible condition.

  4. 04

    Calculate the delivered cost

    Include item price, delivery, service charges, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties before paying.

06 / Clear answers

Before you browse.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy of the linked listings, sizing information, photos and costs.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent product discovery guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, and it does not claim an affiliation with Oopbuy or the brands mentioned.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

This page is a browsing guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet. Use its category links to explore CAFANS listings.

Does a product photo prove authenticity?

No. A product photo or brand name alone does not prove authenticity. Check the seller, listing details, provenance and applicable buyer protections before paying.

How should I compare total shopping costs?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm current charges with the relevant seller or service.
